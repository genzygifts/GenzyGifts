Return & Refund Policy

We accept returns only if the product is damaged or defective. The request must be made within 24 hours of delivery.

  • Returns are not accepted for “change of mind.”
  • To process a return, you must provide an unboxing video as proof of damage.
  • Refunds will be issued within 5–7 working days after product inspection.

Contact us via WhatsApp or email for assistance with returns or issues.
